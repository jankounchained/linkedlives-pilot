<objective>
Build `task1_handicaps/harmonise.py`, the Task 1 script. It turns the pooled `handicaps` values of the five Link-Lives censuses into File A (a complete value-level mapping with proposed standard, confidence and suggested category) and File B (the 300 highest-impact clusters for Asbjørn plus 5 seeded example records per cluster). The category suggestions are written once by the executor in-session into a committed lookup.

Purpose: HARM-01..HARM-04 and ROADMAP Phase 2 criteria 2 and 4. Every method choice is already locked in CONTEXT.md (D-01..D-16); this plan implements them with no extra tuning knobs. The user's guiding preference is "minimise analyst degrees of freedom": the only constants are REF_THRESHOLD (Phase 1 value), the brief's review-capacity numbers, EXAMPLES_PER_CLUSTER and SEED.

Output: committed `task1_handicaps/harmonise.py`, `task1_handicaps/outputs/handicaps_mapping.parquet`, `task1_handicaps/category_suggestions.parquet`, and the `OUTPUTS_DIR` addition to `scripts/project_paths.py`. Local only (gitignored, never committed, D-15): `data/outputs/task1/handicaps_review.{parquet,csv}`, `data/outputs/task1/handicaps_review_records.{parquet,csv}`, `data/outputs/task1/review_leaders.parquet`.

**Data-handling rule (CLAUDE.md, D-15, Phase 1 D-02):** never print census records or pa_id rows into the transcript. While debugging, inspect schemas, shapes, null counts and value-level aggregates only. The handicaps value strings themselves (leaders, merged values, counts) are the variable under study and may be viewed; never view them next to person attributes. Never open handicaps_review_records.* in the session.
