## Decisions Made

- Negations (`nej`, `nei`, `ingen`, `intet`, `rask`, `normal`, `ingen svaghed`) and pauper, household and occupation values all take "not a condition (suspected misplaced)". The fixed eight-label vocabulary has no negation bucket; the README should say this label also holds explicit "no condition" answers.
- Intellectual-disability terms (`aandsvag`, `aandssvag`, `åndsvag`, `fjollet`, `idiot`, every `forstanden berøvet ...`) go to "uden forstandsevner/idiot" (D-09 names `forstanden berøvet` explicitly). Acquired-versus-congenital onset is not separated.
- Epilepsy alone (`epeleptiker`) goes to other/unclear because the census vocabulary has no group for it; combined with another condition it counts toward "multiple conditions".
- Physical terms (`invalid`, `krigsinvalid`, `lam`, `halt`, `dværg`) go to "krøbling/vanfør"; general weakness (`svagelig`, `legemssvag`, `gigtsvag`, `syg`, `patient ...`) goes to other/unclear.
- Lookup authoring used ordered keyword rules in a throwaway scratchpad script, then a by-eye review of every label listing with explicit overrides (`stum` to døvstum/døv, `uarbejdsdygtig` and `patient på sygehuset` to other/unclear). The builder is not tracked; the committed parquet is the audit trail.
